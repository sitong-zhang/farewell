Explain in one sentence what this site is for Positioning

The core job of a website (as opposed to a Web App) is to persuade and guide: within 5 seconds the visitor should know who you are, how you can help, and which button to click.

  • Write the headline as an outcome rather than a capability: "Build a data dashboard in 3 minutes" beats "A professional BI platform".
  • Three things above the fold: value proposition / social proof / one primary CTA.
  • Keep navigation items to 5–7; move anything beyond that into "More" or the footer.
  • Each page solves one job: the homepage persuades, the pricing page converts, the docs page lowers the barrier, the blog captures search traffic.

Above the fold: the densest information sits above the fold line Above the fold

  • Key information must be visible without scrolling: headline, subhead, CTA and credibility evidence.
  • Target an LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) of < 2.5s; don't put a large autoplaying video above the fold.
  • Use a solid high-contrast button for the CTA and keep only one primary CTA on the page; secondary actions use ghost buttons.
  • When the navbar sticks to the top, compress it to 56–64px so it doesn't eat too much of the viewport.

Typography: good design is design people can read Typography

  • Body measure of 60–75 characters (30–40 Chinese characters), with paragraph spacing of at least 1.5× the font size.
  • Heading steps must contrast clearly: H1 32–48 / H2 24–32 / H3 20–24, with adjacent levels differing by at least 1.25×.
  • Don't use pure black #000 for body text; use something like #1F2430; line height 1.6–1.75.
  • In mixed Chinese-English text, add spaces on both sides of the English; use full-width punctuation; align important numbers with tabular-nums.

CTA and conversion buttons Conversion

  • Verb + benefit: "Generate icons for free" converts far better than "Submit".
  • Button height ≥ 44px (48 on mobile), horizontal padding ≥ 24px.
  • Never show two buttons of equal weight on the same screen; repeat a CTA at least one full screen apart.
  • Only ask for the form fields you actually need: every extra field costs a layer of conversion.

Navigation structure: don't make users hunt for the way Navigation

  • The top bar is for "global", the sidebar is for "current context", breadcrumbs are for "hierarchy".
  • Add an anchor table of contents to long pages (this page has one at the top) and highlight the current section.
  • The footer is not a trash can: list 3–5 groups for product / resources / company / legal, plus copyright and filing information.
  • Keep the CTA in the mobile menu; don't collapse away the conversion entry point.

Images, illustrations and video Media

  • Unify ratios: 16:9 or 4:3 for card images, 1:1 for avatars, and leave enough margin around illustrations.
  • Always set width/height or aspect-ratio, otherwise the layout jumps as images load (CLS).
  • Never bake text into images — that loses on SEO, translation and accessibility all at once.
  • For video, provide a poster frame + muted autoplay + explicit playback controls.

Motion: 200–300ms is enough Motion

  • Interface motion should be 150–300ms; beyond 400ms it visibly feels sluggish.
  • Use cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) or ease-out for easing, never linear.
  • Entrance animations need translation + fade-in; pure fade-in looks aimless; stagger list items by 30–50ms.
  • Support @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) to turn animations off.

SEO and sharing SEO

  • A unique <title> (≤ 60 characters) and description (≤ 160 characters) for every page.
  • Open Graph / Twitter Card preview images at 1200×630, otherwise shared links show up as a gray block.
  • canonical for deduplication, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and structured data (Product / Article / FAQ).
  • Only one H1 per page, and don't skip heading levels (jumping from H2 straight to H4 is an error).

Compliance and trust Compliance

  • HTTPS across the whole site; add rel="noopener" to external links (all outbound links in this repo already do).
  • Cookie / tracking notices: give "Reject" and "Accept" equal weight; don't offer only "Agree".
  • Privacy policy, terms and contact details must be reachable; sites in China should watch for ICP filing and cybersecurity grading requirements.
  • When displaying logos, respect trademark status: brand assets are often CC0/CC-BY licensed, but trademark rights don't transfer with the license.
